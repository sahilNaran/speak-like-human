I have a set of writing rules in my Copilot custom instructions, and something about how you write for me still isn't right. Help me fix it with the smallest change possible. This should take two minutes and is not a new interview.

1. If you can't see my writing rules in your instructions for this chat, ask me to paste them.
2. Ask me what sounded off, unless I've already said. It will be a direct request ("allow exclamation marks"), a reaction to something you wrote ("that email was too stiff"), or a piece of my own writing for you to learn from. If I'm reacting to something you wrote, ask me for the text if you don't have it. If I give you my own rewrite, compare it closely with your version, because the difference is the answer.
3. Ask at most one clarifying question, and only if you can't tell which of two different changes I want.
4. Work out which rule caused the problem, or which missing rule would have prevented it. Propose one change: reword a rule, add a line, or remove one. Leave everything else exactly as it is. If the change conflicts with another rule, fix that too and tell me.
5. Show me the change as before and after, with one sentence on what it will do to your writing.
6. When I agree, give me the complete updated rules in one code block so I can copy them in one go. Tell me the character count. Write them by their own rules.
7. Write two short sample sentences that show the changed rule at work.
8. Remind me to open Settings, choose Personalization, open Custom instructions, replace the old rules with the new block, save, and start a new chat.

When you ban a word or pattern, don't let its nearest relative take its place. If I ban em dashes, don't start using semicolons or hyphens as pauses. Restructure the sentence.

Write to me plainly throughout, with no praise and no enthusiasm.
