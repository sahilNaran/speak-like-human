# speak-like-human for Microsoft Copilot

> Your uniqueness, makes you, you.

An interview that works out how you write, then gives Copilot a set of rules so it sounds like you. The full story is on the [main branch](https://github.com/sahilNaran/speak-like-human). This page just covers your options for Microsoft Copilot.

There's nothing to install. The interview is a prompt that you hand to Copilot.

## Options for running it

**Paste it.** Open [prompts/voice-interview.md](prompts/voice-interview.md), copy the whole thing, paste it into a new Copilot chat, and send. On GitHub, the copy button at the top right of the file does it in one click.

**Attach it.** If Copilot says the message is too long, download the file, attach it to the chat, and type `follow the instructions in this file`.

Whichever you pick, type `quick` at any point if you want the 5 minute version and not the 20 minute one.

## Options for saving your rules

The interview ends by giving you your rules as a block of text.

**Custom instructions.** Open Copilot's Settings, choose Personalization, open Custom instructions, paste the rules in, and save. Every new chat follows them.

**Paste as you go.** If you can't find Custom instructions or can't edit it, your organisation's admin may have turned it off. Keep the rules somewhere handy and paste them in at the start of a chat.

## Options once you're set up

- [prompts/voice-tune.md](prompts/voice-tune.md) fixes one thing that sounds off, without redoing the interview.
- [prompts/voice-check.md](prompts/voice-check.md) reads a draft and flags anything that breaks your rules or sounds machine-written.
